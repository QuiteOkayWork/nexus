# PROJECT

Create a premium one-page product website for an AI Decision Intelligence platform called **NEXUS**.

NEXUS helps leadership, strategy, finance, operations, and analyst teams turn fragmented business signals into clear, evidence-backed decisions.

The entire website must feel like a sophisticated intelligence report combined with a modern enterprise product interface.

The visual direction must closely follow the two provided references:

* Dark editorial layouts.
* Precise grid systems.
* Controlled blue atmospheric gradients.
* Sparse glassmorphism.
* Thin borders.
* Strong numerical hierarchy.
* Radial data visualisations.
* Technical annotations.
* Asymmetric layouts.
* Restrained typography.
* High contrast.
* Premium experimental interface design.

DO NOT create a generic SaaS landing page.

DO NOT create a conventional startup design with large hero text, rounded cards, colourful icons, feature grids, gradient buttons, testimonials, pricing cards, or oversized typography.

DO NOT make every element glassmorphic.

DO NOT use huge typography everywhere.

Large typography is allowed ONLY when a number, percentage, metric, or one particularly important decision needs strong visual emphasis.

The interface must feel analytical, intelligent, controlled, and expensive.

---

# CORE PRODUCT POSITIONING

Product name:

**NEXUS**

Product category:

**AI Decision Intelligence**

Core proposition:

**Turn fragmented signals into clear decisions.**

NEXUS continuously analyses business, customer, market, financial, operational, and competitive signals.

It identifies what is changing, connects related evidence, evaluates possible actions, exposes uncertainty, and recommends the strongest next move.

The product must NOT feel like a chatbot.

It must feel like an intelligence system.

The website must communicate this transformation:

**Signal → Context → Scenario → Decision → Confidence → Outcome**

# GLOBAL VISUAL SYSTEM

## Background

Use predominantly:

* Near-black.
* Charcoal black.
* Extremely dark navy.

Avoid pure flat black across the entire page.

Introduce controlled atmospheric gradients in selected areas.

Primary gradient colours:

* Deep navy blue.
* Cool electric blue.
* Muted steel blue.
* Very subtle cyan.
* Very subtle desaturated olive only where useful.

Do NOT use purple-heavy AI gradients.

Do NOT use neon rainbow gradients.

Do NOT make gradients decorative blobs.

Gradients must behave like controlled directional light entering a dark environment.

The reference feeling should be closer to:

A dark intelligence room illuminated by blue analytical light.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Use a clean modern sans-serif / grotesk typography system.

Typography must remain restrained.

Headings should generally be medium-sized.

Do NOT make standard section headings fill the viewport.

Do NOT use giant 100–150px headings simply because space exists.

Desktop approximate hierarchy:

* Hero heading: approximately 48–64px maximum.
* Section headings: approximately 34–48px.
* Supporting headings: approximately 20–28px.
* Body: approximately 14–18px.
* Labels / metadata: approximately 10–12px.
* Important numerical values may be significantly larger when justified.

Numbers may reach approximately 80–140px ONLY when the number is the actual visual subject of the composition.

Examples where large numerical typography is appropriate:

**84%**

**13.3%**

**72 / 100**

**+18.9%**

Do not apply the same scale to normal sentences.

Use generous but controlled line-height.

Do not use ultra-bold typography everywhere.

Prefer Regular, Medium, and Semibold weights.

---

# GRID LANGUAGE

The grid is a major visual component.

Use thin structural lines throughout the page.

Lines may divide:

* Sections.
* Metrics.
* Content blocks.
* Data areas.
* Visualisation areas.

The grid should sometimes resemble an editorial report.

Use:

* Thin 1px borders.
* Very low-opacity white lines.
* Occasional blue-tinted lines.
* Unequal column widths.
* Unequal row heights.
* Large blank cells.
* Small metadata attached to grid intersections.

Avoid repeated identical cards.

Whenever possible, allow the PAGE GRID itself to contain the information.

---

# GLASSMORPHISM

Glassmorphism must be restrained.

Glass should be used only for important intelligence surfaces.

Use:

* Dark translucent panels.
* Subtle backdrop blur.
* Thin translucent border.
* Faint blue illumination.
* Minimal shadow.
* Slight inner highlight.

Do NOT make every section a glass card.

Do NOT create floating rounded rectangles everywhere.

Border radius should remain controlled.

Most containers should use:

* 8px.
* 12px.
* Maximum around 16px.

Avoid excessive 30px–50px rounded corners.

# RESPONSIVE RULES

The website must remain fully designed from approximately 1920px down to 320px.

Do NOT simply stack desktop cards vertically.

On smaller screens:

* Reduce grid complexity.
* Preserve information hierarchy.
* Keep important numbers prominent.
* Simplify radial visualisations.
* Remove non-essential technical annotations.
* Preserve thin editorial dividers.
* Convert wide tables into structured rows.
* Keep typography restrained.
* Maintain adequate spacing.
* Keep decision content readable.
* Prevent horizontal overflow.

The mobile version must retain the editorial intelligence aesthetic.
